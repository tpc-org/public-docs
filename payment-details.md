This guide is for the person at your organization who handles finance
or payments. It covers how to submit your banking details so Hola AI
can pay out your revenue.

## Where to add your details

1. Log into the [Hola AI dashboard](https://admin.sayhola.ai) with your
   account.
2. Go to **Account settings**.
3. Look for the **Banking details** section under your organization's
   name.

If you don't see this section, your account doesn't currently have
access to it — ask whoever set up your Hola AI account (an account
Owner) to grant you access, or fill it in themselves.

## What you'll need

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Legal entity name | The registered legal name of your business |
| Country of incorporation | Where your business is legally registered |
| Registered business address | Your business's official registered address |
| Finance/payments contact name | Who Hola AI should contact about payments |
| Finance/payments email | Their email address |
| Bank account country | The country where your bank account is held |
| Account beneficiary / holder name | The name on the bank account (usually your legal entity name) |
| Bank name | Your bank's name |
| Account number / IBAN | Your account number or IBAN |
| SWIFT / BIC | Your bank's SWIFT/BIC code |
| Local bank routing code | Your bank's local routing number/sort code, if applicable |
| Additional payment instructions | Anything else your bank needs to receive a wire (optional) |
| Authorized representative name | Name of the person authorizing these payment instructions |
| Title / position | Their title at your organization |

**Hola AI only pays out in USD.** This is fixed and can't be changed
per account — if your bank account is in a different currency, your
bank will handle the conversion on receipt.

## Security

- Your account number and routing code are **never displayed back to
  you in full** after you save them — the form only ever shows the
  last 4 digits, as confirmation that something's on file. If you need
  to correct either, just re-enter the whole value; you don't need to
  see the old one first.
- Only your account's Owner (and whoever they've specifically granted
  access to) can view or edit this section — not every team member
  with dashboard access automatically sees it.
- Any change to your banking details triggers an internal review on
  Hola AI's side, so a change you didn't make or don't recognize will
  be caught quickly. If you ever see a notification about a change you
  didn't make, contact your account manager immediately.

## After you submit

There's no separate "confirm" step — Hola AI's team reviews submitted
details as part of setting up your payout. If anything's missing or
needs clarification, your account manager will follow up.

You can update your details at any time by returning to the same
Account settings page and saving again.

## Contact

For questions about payment setup or payout timing, contact your Hola
AI account manager.
